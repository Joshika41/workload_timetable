Universal University Academic Workload & Timetable ERP

1. Purpose and Vision
The proposed application is a universal, institution-wide ERP portal for SRM University focused on academic workload allocation, workload management, curriculum allocation, and timetable generation and management.
The system is intended to replace fragmented/manual academic workload and timetable processes with a live, dynamic, centralized application that can serve numerous departments, programs, hundreds of faculty members, department coordinators/HoDs, administrators, and senior academic stakeholders.
The application must remain configurable because academic structures, workload requirements, faculty assignments, subjects, rooms, timings, and timetable rules may differ between departments, programs, semesters, academic years, and faculty members.
1.1 Core Objectives
Provide a centralized workload and timetable platform for the whole institution.
Allow authorized administrators to manage academic and faculty data.
Allow faculty to submit subject preferences through a willingness-form-style workflow.
Allow administrators to review, approve, keep pending, deny, and ultimately allocate subjects to faculty.
Generate faculty workload information from finalized allocations.
Simultaneously generate the Class-Wise Curriculum Allocation Matrix from the same allocation data.
Generate conflict-free class/section timetables and individual faculty timetables using configured academic rules and constraints.
Provide live access to workload, assigned subjects, and personal timetables.
Preserve historical records and changes across departments, faculty, allocations, workload, and timetables.
Provide PDF export of generated workload information.
