SRM UNIVERSITY

WORKLOAD AND TIMETABLE MANAGEMENT ERP

Functional Requirements and Workflow Specification

1. Login and User Profile Selection

The login page shall provide a simple and common login structure for both HOD and Faculty users. The login process must not contain an academic context selector at the initial stage.

1.1 Login Profile Selection

The first step of the login process shall allow the user to select their login profile from two options:

1. HOD


2. Faculty



The user must first select whether they are entering the system as an HOD or as a Faculty member.

1.2 Institution Selection

After selecting the login profile, the user shall select their institution from a dropdown list.

The dropdown must contain the four institutions available in the ERP:

1. E&T


2. Flabs


3. Management


4. B-Arch



1.3 User Profile Selection

After selecting the institution, the user shall select their individual profile from the profile dropdown.

The profile dropdown must display the relevant users based on the previously selected login role and institution.

Each profile entry should clearly provide:

Faculty/HOD Name

Employee ID

Institution

Department

Relevant designation/seniority information


The profiles should be arranged according to seniority.

1.4 Role-Based Profile Filtering

The profile dropdown must dynamically apply role-based filtering.

For example:

If the user selects HOD, only HOD profiles belonging to the selected institution shall be displayed.

If the user selects Faculty, only Faculty profiles belonging to the selected institution shall be displayed.


The system must therefore prevent unrelated profiles from appearing in the selection list.

1.5 Password and Sign-In

For the current ERP implementation/demo, the password shall be automatically populated with:

123456

The sign-in button must dynamically display the selected role.

Examples:

SIGN IN AS HOD

SIGN IN AS FACULTY


This login structure and filtering logic shall be common across the entire ERP.

2. HOD Portal

After an HOD successfully logs into the system, the HOD shall be taken to the dashboard/home page corresponding to their department.

The HOD must be able to view and manage the faculty members belonging to their department.

2.1 Department Faculty Listing

The HOD home page shall display the complete faculty list of the HOD's department.

The faculty list must:

Display faculty members neatly.

Be arranged according to seniority.

Show relevant faculty information.

Provide a View Details action for every faculty member.


The HOD must only be able to access faculty information belonging to the appropriate department and academic context.


---

3. Faculty Details and Subject Allocation

When the HOD selects View Details for a faculty member, the system shall open a clear and structured allocation screen.

The allocation information shall be displayed in tabular form.

3.1 Allocation Table

The table shall contain the following columns:

ColumnInformation

1Subject Preference and Class Type
2Approval
3Section
4Role
5Theory Hours
6Lab Hours
7Total Hours


3.2 Subject and Class Type

The first column shall display the subjects selected/preferred by the faculty member.

Each subject must also display its corresponding class type.

This is particularly important where different programmes or class types exist within the same department.

For example:

MCA General

MCA GEN AI


The HOD must be able to clearly understand which class type the subject belongs to.

3.3 Approval

The HOD shall be able to approve or reject a faculty member's subject preference.

The approval field shall be presented as a dropdown containing:

Yes

No


The approval decision must be associated with the specific subject preference.

3.4 Section Allocation

The HOD shall select the section to which the faculty member will be allocated.

The section shall be selected through a dropdown.

For example:

Section A

Section B

Section C


The available sections must be generated from the actual academic context rather than being permanently hardcoded.

3.5 Faculty Role Allocation

The HOD shall assign the faculty member a role for the respective subject.

The available roles shall be:

1. Incharge-1


2. Incharge-2



The role must be selected independently for each applicable subject allocation.

3.6 Theory and Lab Hours

The HOD shall manually allocate teaching hours.

Two separate hour fields shall be provided:

Theory Hours

Lab Hours


Both shall use dropdown selections ranging from:

1 to 10 hours

The HOD must select the appropriate number of hours for each type.

3.7 Total Hours

The system shall automatically calculate the total allocated hours.

The total must be derived from the allocated theory and lab hours rather than being entered manually.

For example:

Theory Hours + Lab Hours = Total Allocated Hours

The calculated total shall be displayed clearly in the allocation table.

4. HOD Allocation Constraints and Business Rules

The following rules must be enforced throughout the HOD allocation process.

4.1 Seniority-Based Faculty Listing

Faculty members displayed within the HOD portal must be arranged according to their seniority.

This ordering must be generated dynamically from the faculty information stored in the system.

4.2 Subject Allocation Conflict

Multiple faculty members may initially select/prefer the same subject.

For example:

Faculty A → Subject X

Faculty B → Subject X


Both preferences may exist initially.

However, once the HOD officially allocates and finalizes that subject to a faculty member in the applicable academic context, the same subject must no longer be available for allocation to another faculty member where the allocation would create a conflict.

The previously selected subject should therefore become appropriately disabled for the other faculty member.

This restriction must be applied based on the actual academic context and must not incorrectly disable the subject globally across unrelated departments, programmes, semesters, or class types.

4.3 Faculty Workload Hour Cap

Each faculty member shall have a maximum workload/hour cap based on their designation.

The system must ensure:

Allocated Hours ≤ Faculty Designation Hour Cap

The allocated workload may be lower than the faculty member's maximum allowed workload.

However, the system must never allow the allocation to exceed the applicable hour cap.

The hour cap must therefore be dynamically associated with the faculty member's designation rather than using one universal limit for everyone.


---

5. Workload Finalization

Once the HOD has completed the required allocations for the faculty members in the relevant academic context, the system shall allow the workload to be finalized.

The Finalize Workload button shall only become available when the required allocation conditions have been satisfied.

After successful finalization:

The allocation becomes the official workload data.

The finalized workload becomes available to the relevant workload views.

Invalid or conflicting allocations must not be finalized.

The system must preserve the integrity of the finalized data.


5.1 PDF Export

Once the workload has been successfully finalized, the Export Workload as PDF option shall become available.

The generated PDF must contain the actual finalized workload data from the system.

The PDF must not contain hardcoded or manually fabricated faculty, subject, section, or hour information.


---

6. Global ERP Applicability

The above HOD workflow and business rules shall not be limited to a single department, institution, or semester.

The same system logic must operate globally throughout the ERP.

The rules shall apply across:

All four institutions

All departments

All programmes

All academic years

All semesters

All sections

All faculty members

All HOD accounts


For example, a rule implemented for faculty workload validation within one department must also apply to equivalent faculty allocation scenarios in other departments and institutions.

The ERP must therefore use dynamic, database-driven rules rather than department-specific or hardcoded logic.

9. Faculty Subject Selection

When a faculty member selects a particular semester, the system shall display the subjects belonging to that semester.

The subject information must clearly identify the class/programme to which each subject belongs.

This is especially important for the MCA department, where two major class types exist:

1. MCA General / MCA


2. MCA GEN AI



The faculty member must be able to see subjects belonging to both class types, with the class type clearly displayed alongside each subject.

Example

If a faculty member selects Semester 3, the system should display the relevant subjects for:

MCA General – 2nd Year

MCA GEN AI – 2nd Year


Each subject must clearly indicate the class type it belongs to.

This prevents ambiguity when similar or related subjects exist across the two MCA divisions.

10. Subject Selection Interface

Faculty members shall be able to select their preferred subjects using an easy checklist-style interface.

Each subject shall have a selection control, such as a checkbox/tick mark.

The faculty member shall be able to:

1. View the subjects.


2. Identify the corresponding class type.


3. Select the required subjects.


4. Review their selections.


5. Submit their preferences to the HOD.



The same functionality must be available for both Odd and Even semester programmes.

11. Faculty Subject Preference Rules

The subject preference rules must be displayed prominently at the top of the subject selection screen.

The rules should be written in simple language so that faculty members understand the requirements before making their selections.

11.1 Class Coordinator Core Subject Requirement

If a faculty member is the Class Coordinator for a particular class type, they must select at least one Core subject belonging to that class type.

For example, if a faculty member is the Class Coordinator for MCA GEN AI, they must select at least one Core subject from the MCA GEN AI class type.

11.2 Core and Elective Requirement

For each selected semester, a faculty member must select:

At least one Core subject

At least one Elective subject


This rule applies separately to each applicable semester.

For example, under an Odd Semester programme, if the faculty member is selecting subjects for Semester 1 and Semester 3, the applicable selection requirements must be enforced for those semesters.

The same principle applies to the Even Semester programme.


---

12. Minor and Multidisciplinary Courses

Minor courses and Multidisciplinary courses shall be treated as optional selections.

These subjects must be clearly marked with an:

(Optional)

tag alongside the course name.

Faculty members may choose these subjects if they wish to do so.

They should not be treated as mandatory requirements for satisfying the Core + Elective selection rule.


---

13. Subject Selection Restriction After Required Selection

For each semester, once the faculty member has selected:

At least one Core subject, and

At least one Elective subject,


the remaining Core and Elective subjects for that semester should become disabled from further selection.

However:

Minor courses remain selectable.

Multidisciplinary courses remain selectable.

Minor and Multidisciplinary courses continue to display the Optional designation.


This rule ensures that the faculty member selects the required combination without unnecessarily selecting additional Core or Elective subjects.

Example

For Semester 3:

1. Faculty selects one Core subject.


2. Faculty selects one Elective subject.


3. Remaining Core subjects become disabled.


4. Remaining Elective subjects become disabled.


5. Minor (Optional) remains selectable.


6. Multidisciplinary (Optional) remains selectable.



This behaviour must be applied consistently across all semesters.

18. Core System Principle

The ERP must function as a single, dynamic, database-driven system.

The rules described in this document must not be implemented only for the current MCA example. MCA General and MCA GEN AI are examples used to explain the requirement.

The same underlying system must be capable of handling different institutions, departments, programmes, class types, semesters, subjects, sections, faculty members, and HODs without requiring separate hardcoded logic for each one.

The fundamental workflow is:

Faculty Preference → HOD Review → HOD Allocation → Validation → Workload Finalization → Official Workload → PDF Export

A faculty member's preference must never automatically become an allocation, and only successfully finalized allocation data should be treated as the official workload of the ERP.
