# How-To Guide: Workload ERP

This manual explains how to use the Workload ERP for both HODs and Faculty members.

## General: Seamless Login
Our ERP uses a seamless "No Credential" login system designed for speed and ease of use.
1. Navigate to the portal homepage (e.g., `http://localhost:8080`).
2. **Context Selection**:
   - **Department**: Select your department (e.g., "MCA & MCA GEN AI").
   - **Program Type**: Select the program (e.g., "Postgraduate").
   - **Semester Type**: Select either "Odd Semester" or "Even Semester".
3. **Role Selection**:
   - Click **Enter as HOD** to access the HOD dashboard.
   - Click **Enter as Faculty** to access the Faculty preference portal.
   *Note: You do not need to enter an email or password. The system seamlessly logs you in based on your chosen context!*

---

## Guide for Faculty Members

### 1. Viewing Available Subjects
- Once you enter as Faculty, your **Faculty Home** will display all semesters active for your chosen context.
- Click on a semester tile (e.g., "Semester 1") to expand it and view the list of available subjects.
- You will see the actual subject codes, titles, and L-T-P-C credits sourced directly from the official curriculum.

### 2. Submitting Subject Preferences
- Review the list of subjects available for the semester.
- Click the **checkbox** next to the subjects you wish to teach.
- Click the **Submit Preferences** button at the bottom of the list.
- Your selected subjects will immediately be sent to the HOD for review. The status will update to **"Submitted"**.

### 3. Checking Allocation Status
- You can check back on this screen to see the status of your preferences.
- If the HOD approves and allocates a subject to you, it will display a green **"Allocated"** badge.

## Guide for Head of Department (HOD)

### 1. Dashboard Overview
- Upon entering as HOD, you will see the **HOD Dashboard**.
- The dashboard summarizes how many faculty have submitted preferences and how many are still pending.

### 2. Reviewing Faculty Preferences
- Navigate to the **Preference Management** page from the sidebar.
- On the left panel, you will see all faculty members in your department. 
- Click on any faculty member's name. Their submitted subject preferences will appear on the right panel.

### 3. Approving Submissions
- If a faculty member has submitted preferences, click the **✓ Approve Submission** button.
- This formally accepts their preferences into the allocation workflow.

### 4. Allocating Workload
- Under each preferred subject, click **📌 Allocate**.
- A small form will appear. You must assign:
  - **Section**: Which section the faculty will teach.
  - **Role**: Main In-charge or Assistant.
  - **Theory/Practical Hours**: The specific hour load assigned.
- Click **Submit Allocation**.
- *Note: If a faculty member did not select a subject they need to teach, you can manually assign them one using the "➕ Offer Additional Subject (HOD)" dropdown.*

### 5. Managing "No Teaching" Faculty
- If a faculty member is on leave or has no teaching load this semester, select them from the list and click the **🔕 No Teaching** button. Provide a reason (e.g., "Sabbatical").

### 6. Finalizing the Workload
- Continue allocating subjects to all faculty members. 
- The circular **Progress Sphere** at the top of the page tracks your progress.
- Once *every* faculty member is either "Allocated" or "No Teaching", the **🏁 Finalize Workload** button at the bottom of the page will unlock.
- Click it to finalize the department's workload for the semester. Once finalized, preferences and allocations are locked.
